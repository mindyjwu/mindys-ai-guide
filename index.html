How do I text my daughter about her dating life without sounding too nosy? I want to show I care but not scare her off 😅
AI
Two approaches depending on your vibe:

Option 1 — The Casual Check-in:
"Hey! Just thinking about you. How's everything going? Life, work, all of it 😊"
Opens the door without zeroing in — she can share as much or as little as she wants.

Option 2 — The Gentle Opener:
"I know I'm your mom and that's automatic cringe, but I'm always here if you ever want to talk about anything. No judgment, promise 🤍"
Acknowledges the awkwardness with humour — kids often respond better when you don't pretend it isn't weird.
